<div data-lang-block="en" markdown="1">

**Authors:** **Kai Ren**, Yuichi Nakamura, Kazuaki Kondo, Kei Shimonishi, Qi An, Takahide Ito, Jun-ichiro Furukawa, Masashi Toda and Junichi Akita

**Status:** Published in *Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology* (2026). DOI: 10.3389/fbioe.2026.1792582.

This manuscript presents a unified real-time framework for anticipatory EMG-based prediction of sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit transitions. The work aims to support timely and reliable motion intention estimation for assistive and interactive systems based on physiological signals.

</div>

**作者：** **Kai Ren**, Yuichi Nakamura, Kazuaki Kondo, Kei Shimonishi, Qi An, Takahide Ito, Jun-ichiro Furukawa, Masashi Toda and Junichi Akita

**状态：** 发表于 *Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology* (2026)。DOI: 10.3389/fbioe.2026.1792582。

该论文提出了一个统一的实时框架，用于基于肌电信号提前预测坐站和站坐转换。该研究旨在为基于生理信号的辅助和交互系统提供及时、可靠的动作意图估计。
