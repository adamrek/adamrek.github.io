**Authors:** **Kai Ren**, Yuichi Nakamura, Kazuaki Kondo, Kei Shimonishi, Qi An, Takahide Ito, Jun-ichiro Furukawa, Masashi Toda and Junichi Akita

**Status:** Accepted for publication in *Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology* (2026).

This manuscript presents a unified real-time framework for anticipatory EMG-based prediction of sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit transitions. The work aims to support timely and reliable motion intention estimation for assistive and interactive systems based on physiological signals.
