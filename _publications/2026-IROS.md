**Authors:** **Kai Ren**, Yuichi Nakamura, Takahide Ito, Kazuaki Kondo, Kei Shimonishi, Qi An 

**Status:** Manuscript under review.

This manuscript presents a practical framework for motion classification and forecasting toward safer assistive support in sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit motions. The work aims to improve the reliability and safety of assistive systems through real-time motion understanding and prediction.
