## Research Focus

<div class="home-grid">
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>Physiological Signal Modeling</h3>
    <p>EMG acquisition, preprocessing, feature extraction, and deep time-series modeling for understanding human movement from muscle activity.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>Motion Intention Prediction</h3>
    <p>Anticipatory classification and forecasting of sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit transitions, including safety-aware motion state estimation.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>Assistive Interactive Systems</h3>
    <p>Real-time inference pipelines, closed-loop experimental systems, and integration with chair-based and wearable assistive prototypes.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## 研究方向

<div class="home-grid">
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>生理信号建模</h3>
    <p>围绕肌电采集、预处理、特征提取和深度时序建模，从肌肉活动中理解人体运动。</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>动作意图预测</h3>
    <p>针对坐站转换和站坐转换进行前瞻性分类与预测，并结合安全感知的运动状态估计。</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>辅助型交互系统</h3>
    <p>构建实时推理流程、闭环实验系统，并集成到座椅式和可穿戴辅助原型中。</p>
  </section>
