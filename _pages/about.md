## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at **Kyoto University**, based in Kyoto, Japan.

My research focuses on **electromyography (EMG) sensing**, **deep learning**, and **human–computer interaction (HCI)**. I work on building learning methods and interactive systems that can extract meaningful information from physiological signals and support more natural human–machine interaction. My work spans the full pipeline from EMG sensing hardware and signal acquisition to neural network modeling, real-time inference, and integration with assistive interactive systems.

My broader interests include **signal processing**, **machine learning**, **multimodal sensing**, and **human-centered AI**. Through my research, I aim to bridge physiological signal understanding with practical interactive applications.

I am currently interested in **research collaborations** and future opportunities in **research science**, **research engineering**, and **applied machine learning**, especially in areas related to physiological sensing, multimodal learning, and intelligent interfaces.

## 关于我

我目前是**京都大学**博士研究生，现居日本京都。

我的研究主要集中在**肌电（EMG）感知**、**深度学习**和**人机交互（HCI）**。我致力于构建能够从生理信号中提取有效信息的学习方法与交互系统，以支持更加自然的人机交互。我的研究覆盖从 EMG 感知硬件与信号采集，到神经网络建模、实时推理以及辅助交互系统集成的完整技术链路。

更广泛地说，我的兴趣还包括**信号处理**、**机器学习**、**多模态感知**和**人本智能**。我希望通过研究把生理信号理解与实际交互应用连接起来。

我目前也关注与**生理信号感知**、**多模态学习**、**智能交互接口**相关的学术合作，以及未来的研究员、研究工程师和应用机器学习岗位机会。
