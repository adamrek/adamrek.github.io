<div data-lang-block="en" markdown="1">

This research focuses on the analysis of electromyography (EMG) signals for human-machine interaction.

The work investigates machine learning and deep learning approaches for EMG-based sensing and control, with applications to human-robot collaboration and power assist systems.

</div>

<div data-lang-block="zh" markdown="1">

该研究聚焦于面向人机交互的肌电（EMG）信号分析。

研究内容包括基于机器学习和深度学习的肌电感知与控制方法，并将其应用于人机协作和助力辅助系统。
